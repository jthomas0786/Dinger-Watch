Pulse Sports Privacy Notice

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Information we collect

When accounts are enabled, Pulse Sports stores your account identifier, profile handle, followed teams, posts, and predictions. We also collect limited technical error information needed to keep the service reliable.

How we use it

We use this information to provide account features, personalize followed teams, operate community features, prevent abuse, and diagnose service errors. We do not sell personal information.

Sports data

Scores, schedules, and standings are supplied by a licensed data provider. Their terms may also apply to your use of that data.

Retention and choices

You may request deletion of your account data by contacting the operator. Posts or predictions may be retained briefly where necessary for safety, legal, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Security

We use access controls, row-level database security, and rate limiting. No system can guarantee absolute security.
